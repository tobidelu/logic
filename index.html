FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good to know.

What types of cargo can THQ Logistics handle?

We can discuss a wide range of commercial and personal cargo. Share the cargo type, dimensions, weight and destination for a tailored assessment.

How do I request a shipping estimate?

Use our Get a Quote form with your origin, destination, service and shipment details. Your email app will prepare an enquiry.

Can you help with customs documentation?

Customs clearance and documentation support are included in our service offering. Requirements depend on your cargo and destination.

How long will my shipment take?

Transit times vary by route, transport mode and customs processing. Request a quote for a route-specific estimate.
